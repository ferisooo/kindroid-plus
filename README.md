# Kindroid+

Kindroid (kindroid.ai) in its own Android app. Log in once and stay logged in.

**Download:** https://github.com/ferisooo/kindroid-plus/releases/latest/download/KindroidPlus.apk

In a Kin's chat, your Kin's selfie photos, video selfies and your own pictures take turns as
the chat background. Tap the 🖼 button for the order, the speed (1 second to 5 minutes), what to
show, ⭐ favorites only, your own pictures, the message boxes (solid to invisible), and the lock.

✨ AI ideas: add your own AI key (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok or DeepSeek), then tap ✨ on
Kindroid's boxes (scene, personality, key memories…) or 💬 over the message box.

🔒 App lock: PIN, pattern or password. Forgot it? "Forgot?" starts over (log in to Kindroid again).
